# F-1: Step.compensation cannot hold "cancel_work_order"

`contracts/objects.py`: `Step.compensation: ActionName | None`, but `ActionName` has no `cancel_work_order`,
while `contracts/tools.COMPENSATION["create_work_order"] == "cancel_work_order"`. Building a Step for
create_work_order with `compensation=COMPENSATION[action]` raises a pydantic ValidationError.

Proposed (additive): add `"cancel_work_order"` to `ActionName`.
Affects: D (plan construction), C (action dispatch), F (FakeWorld shims it by leaving compensation=None for that step).
